# Decision 002 - Beyond Presence for Avatar Layer

Date: 2026-05-16  
Owner: Echoes Product + Engineering  
Status: Accepted

## Context

Echoes requires a cinematic emotional presence layer that goes beyond text and standard voice agents.

## Decision

Use Beyond Presence as the avatar experience layer, with fallback to voice/text when unavailable.

## Consequences

- stronger emotional differentiation
- increased integration complexity and reliability requirements
- mandatory disclosure and anti-deception UX constraints

## Alternatives Considered

- text/voice only (rejected: weak emotional presence)
- fully custom avatar stack now (deferred due to timeline)
