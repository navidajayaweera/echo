# Product Vision

## Mission

Echoes preserves human identity through memory-grounded AI personas so people can maintain emotional continuity with loved ones, past selves, and family legacy.

## Why Echoes Exists

People lose access to meaningful conversations through death, distance, cognitive decline, or time. Existing AI chat products optimize utility. Echoes optimizes emotional continuity.

## Emotional Philosophy

- Memories are sacred, not disposable content.
- Presence matters more than productivity.
- AI should feel grounded, gentle, and emotionally consistent.
- Identity should be preserved with dignity and consent.

## Core User Problems

- Grief: people want a safe space to reconnect with a loved one's memory.
- Memory decline: people need continuity support for identity and relationships.
- Legacy loss: family stories and values disappear across generations.
- Emotional disconnection: users want context-rich, familiar conversations.

## Primary Use Cases

1. **Lost Loved One**  
   Build a memory-grounded representation from shared artifacts and family-approved inputs.
2. **Dementia / Memory Support**  
   Preserve routines, relationships, and life events to support recall and caregiver workflows.
3. **Future Legacy**  
   Preserve worldview, stories, and advice for future generations.

## What Echoes Is Not

- Not supernatural resurrection.
- Not grief manipulation.
- Not emotional dependency optimization.
- Not a generic chatbot with a memory skin.

## Experience Principles

- Memories should feel sacred.
- AI should feel emotionally grounded.
- Presence over productivity.
- Human-first interactions.
- Never simulate supernatural consciousness.
- Always disclose AI representation clearly.

## Long-Term Vision

Echoes becomes a trusted living archive of identity where families can preserve, revisit, and responsibly share emotionally meaningful human memory across generations.
