# Demo Script

## Objective

Show that Echoes preserves emotional continuity through persona-driven, memory-grounded presence.

## Script

1. Open app on Home dashboard.
2. Enter Personas tab and show:
   - Mom
   - Grandpa
   - Younger Me
3. Open Mom persona detail.
4. Show memory feed and relationships.
5. Launch Presence (voice or avatar mode).
6. Ask: "What was my favorite childhood memory?"
7. Show memory-grounded answer + recalled artifact overlay.
8. End with product line:
   "Echoes preserves the people we never want to lose."

## Demo Guardrails

- include explicit AI disclosure at session start
- avoid overclaiming certainty in recalled details
- keep emotional tone warm and grounded
